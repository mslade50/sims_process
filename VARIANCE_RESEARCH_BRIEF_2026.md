## Working evidence

Shot-level player dispersion improved round-score distribution scores in the existing 34-event study; R1 matchup accuracy was essentially unchanged. Course multipliers are shared within category. Player-specific approach shot-chain shape was promising in a three-event pilot when paired with the existing variance estimate. These findings motivate further testing; they do not establish that variance caused the season drawdown.
